TITLE: Jill Insley

Jill Insley is a British financial journalist who has written for The Guardian and The Sunday Times, including the Question of Money consumer advice column.


== Orlando the cat ==

In a 2012 portfolio stock valuation challenge by The Observer, Jill Insley's pet cat, Orlando, outperformed a team of professional investors. In reaction, Insley wrote "My cat is a genius."

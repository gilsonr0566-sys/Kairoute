TITLE: Furnas

Furnas is a village in the Azores archipelago on the island of São Miguel, and was home to 1,450 people in 2011. It is famous for its geothermal activity. Hot springs and geysers are prevalent, and traditional meals are cooked via thermal energy in pots buried in the ground.


== Understand ==


== Get in ==
From Ponta Delgada take bus C318 or C110 for €4.65 (Jan 2024). It takes about 1 hr 20 min. There are multiple bus stops in the village.


== Get around ==

The village can be easily explored on foot.


== See ==
1 Lagoa das Furnas. A peaceful lake over an active caldera, with many birds, trees, and scenic views. You can sometimes see bubbles coming up from geothermal vents in the lake. There is a trail around the lake: the south and west side of the trail has many stops (an old church, environmental center, statues, etc.). At the north side, you can see some fumaroles and earth ovens where locals and local restaurants cook food in earth ovens. The east side of the trail is right next to the road. South parking lot free, north parking lot €3. (updated Jun 2026)
2 Casa Invertida, Av. Victor Manuel Rodrigues. A house that is upside down. (updated Jan 2024)


== Do ==
1 Poça da Dona Beija, Lomba Das Barracas 1. Hot springs facility featuring thermal pools, tropical landscaping, showers & a gift shop. €8. (updated Jan 2024)
2 Terra Nosta Botanical Garden (Parque Terra Nostra), Largo Marquês da Praia e Monfort. A 200-year-old park featuring local flora and fauna, as well as outdoor mineral baths. Bring an old swimsuit since the iron in the water might stain it. The lockers require you to use an app on your phone, so make sure you have data/signal, and be prepared to bring your phone with you to the baths after locking up. €17 for baths and garden, €5 more for a locker. (updated Jun 2026)

== Eat ==
Many restaurants serve the local specialty cozido, a stew cooked for 8 hours in a volcanic vent. Some of the more popular restaurants recommend a reservation to guarantee a spot.

## Uncertainty register
- Left out: the height of the Athena Parthenos (no source checked gives it); the number killed in 1687 (only one secondary figure); the exact year the Parthenon became a mosque (sources differ, so "within a few decades").
- Elgin dates differ between sources (1800-1803, 1801-1802, 1801-1812), so the script says "in the early 1800s".
- Why the Propylaia stopped is unknown; the script says so.
- Opening hours, ticket rules, daily caps and heat closures change; the script gives none (the app shows current hours). Summer heat can close the site at midday: keep the "water and hat" line.

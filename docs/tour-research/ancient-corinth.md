## Uncertainty register
- Bema: identified by its form and place, not by an inscription; the script says "very likely". A later church on it is not confirmed by the sources checked, so it is left out.
- Gallio's year: sources give 51-52 or 52-53; the script says "around AD 51 to 52" and the letter "about AD 52".
- Erastus: identification disputed; told as a possibility.
- Left out: Strabo's "thousand temple prostitutes" on Acrocorinth (refers to the older Greek city and is debated); an earlier 7th-century temple date (sources conflict).
- Opening hours and tickets change; none in the script. The museum is inside the site.

# Kairoute Idea Garden

Future ideas for Kairoute, researched but **not being built** until Richard picks one. Updated Nov 13, 2026. The full research (how each works, partners, cost, legal and safety, effort) is in the project's Idea Garden doc and in `idea-garden/kairoute-idea-garden.md` in the project files.

All 10 ideas can be built, but they split into three kinds: features we build ourselves on what Kairoute already has, community features that need sign-in first, and partnerships that need a proven app with real users. Start with whisper mode and the pilgrim passport.

Effort: S = days, M = 2 to 4 weeks, L = 1 to 3 months, XL = a partnership taking 6 months or more. Costs are rough planning numbers.

## Rules that apply to every idea

- **Accuracy first.** Nothing invented. "Time machine sound" was replaced by a source-based version because reconstructed sound would break the same rule that dropped AI "then and now" images. Traveler tips are always labeled as traveler reports, never mixed with checked facts.
- **General, not one trip.** Each idea must work for every trip and city, not just the test trip.
- **Shared building blocks:** sign-in, payments, GPS arrival triggers, the phone app wrapper, and a company with insurance and partner terms. Sign-in, payments and audio recording are parked until Richard unparks them.
